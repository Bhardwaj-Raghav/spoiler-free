How it works

Spoiler Free runs only on YouTube in this browser. It reads video titles and blocks (covers) or removes tiles when a title contains text you have saved.

Keywords

Matching is case-insensitive. If your phrase appears anywhere in the title, it counts as a hit (substring match, not the whole title). Add multiple phrases from the popup; each is checked separately.

Remove vs block

Remove drops the whole video row from the page. Block keeps the row but covers it with a solid panel (light or dark to match YouTube) until you use Reveal. On Shorts, playback pauses when a reel is blocked. Changing this may require refreshing open YouTube tabs.

Where it scans

  • Home — home feed and grids, including Shorts shelves mixed into the feed.
  • Search — search results (videos and Shorts-style results).
  • Watch — the right-hand “Up next” column and Shorts rows under the player, including lockup tiles (yt-lockup-view-model / attributed-string titles).
  • Channel — Featured and Videos (and the same tile types there).
  • Subscriptions — the subscriptions feed.

The /shorts vertical feed is always scanned when you open it (same block/remove behavior). Turn a place off above to leave matching videos visible there.

Counts

Shielded increments each time a tile is blocked or removed after a match. Phrases is how many entries you have saved.

Limits

YouTube’s layout changes often; the extension walks the page to find titles. Very new or unusual layouts might be missed. It does not read comments or descriptions—only titles on supported surfaces.
